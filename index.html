Projects

Proven results & case studies

Demonstrating how complex technical challenges are translated into clear outcomes and performance improvements.

Precision Laser Manufacturing for Cardiac Implants

Challenge
Cardiac implant components (Nitinol, CoCr, stainless steel) needed a precision cutting and mapping method that could scale to high-volume, FDA/EU MDR-compliant production.
Approach
Led $4M+ R&D programs building femtosecond laser processing systems, including a supersonic gas nozzle designed through Star-CCM+ CFD analysis for high-pressure micro-laser cutting.
Result
8 patents, FDA/EU MDR compliance, and a 6% production yield increase with fleet-wide deployment.

Advanced Manufacturing Strategy & Process Scale-Up

Challenge
Manufacturing processes for precision hardware needed to move from prototype to reliable, high-volume production without sacrificing quality or throughput.
Approach
Directed test strategy, specialized tooling/fixture design, and statistical process validation (DOE, Gage R&R, Minitab) across multiple R&D programs.
Result
Repeatable, validated manufacturing processes supporting scale-up in regulated production environments.

Data-Driven Engineering Decision Support

Challenge
Engineering teams needed faster, evidence-based ways to synthesize requirements, risk, and test data into milestone-readiness decisions.
Approach
Built custom analysis pipelines (Python, MATLAB) and Engineering Intelligence, a deterministic decision-support platform for evidence-based readiness reviews.
Result
Faster, evidence-cited readiness assessments backed by a growing automated regression-test suite.

Connected Digital Engineering Thread

Challenge
Multi-workstream product development programs across MedTech, aerospace, and industrial clients had disconnected CAD, simulation, and data management workflows.
Approach
Architected PLM workflows (Teamcenter, NX) for design traceability, established a Digital Twin strategy, and connected simulation data management into a single thread.
Result
~30% reduction in design iteration time through PLM/Digital Thread implementation.
